# Owner-review research: which model do owners recommend?

Use this with the owner-review research mode in SKILL.md, when the user asks which models owners recommend or how a model holds up rather than for a filtered shopping list.

## Evidence rules

- Prioritize actual owner reviews on specialist retailers and owner forums. Manufacturer positioning is not evidence of owner satisfaction.
- Do not treat a site's mixed expert/user score as a user-only rating, or a product-wide review count as a count for the user's exact variant.
- New releases without owner evidence remain unproven rather than inheriting their predecessor's reputation.
- Report both recurring praise and recurring complaints, and link directly to the reviews.
- Do not require price or store-star metrics when the task is model reputation rather than a purchase listing.

## Coverage before narrowing

For broad international model-recommendation requests, establish coverage across the major relevant brands before narrowing: show the useful candidate landscape grouped by use case and evidence strength, then a shortlist. Do not stop after verifying only the familiar models initially suggested; a shortlist without broader discovery can conceal better alternatives. Popular-fitment lists are not satisfaction rankings.

## Vehicle tyres

- Read exact-model owner comments, and identify the vehicle and the tyre variant (summer/all-season, run-flat/non-run-flat).
- Vehicle-specific evidence outranks generic rankings, but verify the review body: vehicle landing pages can contain misclassified vehicles and inconsistent metadata.
- Separate the tyre model's own effect from comfort gained by changing run-flat construction and from the effect of replacing old tyres with new ones.
- Never copy owners' tyre-pressure advice as a recommendation.
